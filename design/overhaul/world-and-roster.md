## 1. The setting in one paragraph

**Ferrum Bay, 2041.** A port city that built the machines that replaced it. When
the *Industrial Retirement Act* forced millions of labor robots out of service,
the bots went to the yards and the operators went with them. Laid-off crane
jockeys, welders and line techs started fighting the decommissioned machines in
scrapyard pits for beer money. Twelve years later it is the most-watched sport
in the region: gangs run the pits, a sanctioning body runs the regionals, and
the same corporations that scrapped the robots now sell the parts to fight them.
Every bot in the ring is a piece of someone's old job.

**Tone:** grit with heart. Working-class underdogs, found family in the corner,
corporate polish versus scrap ingenuity. Violence is mechanical, never human —
the crowd cheers when a plate flies off, and goes quiet when a crew loses the
bot they rebuilt for three years.

## 4. Factions (the "sides")

### 4.1 Corporate techs — "the Makers"

Factory-built parts, clean liveries, strict contracts, deep pockets.

| Maker | Origin | Engineering identity | Livery | Motion signature |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sabre Dynamics** | Security and defense contractor | Precision, reliability, premium alloys | Crimson + black, gold pinstripe | Surgical, minimal wasted motion |
| **Kestrel Aerospace** | Drone and airframe maker | Light composites, long reach, fast servos | White + sky blue | Snappy, springy, darting |
| **Hollis Heavy Industries** | Mining and demolition | Thick plate, hydraulics, huge torque | Olive + hazard yellow | Heavy, deliberate, hydraulic lag and hiss |
| **Volta Grid** | Power utility | Capacitors, shock systems, energy efficiency | Violet + electric cyan | Twitchy bursts, crackling idle |

### 4.2 Gang techs — "the Crews"

Stolen, modified and illegal parts. Grudges, betting, and fights you get asked to lose.

| Crew | Turf | Tech | Code | Wants from you |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Rustmouths** | The Yards | Junkline scrap, welded spikes, anything goes | Run Pit Nights; respect is earned with damage | Fight on their card, pay their cut |
| **Copperline Kings** | Copperline | Stolen Volta capacitors, illegal overvolt mods | Wire thieves with a grid-hacker elite | Test their black-market mods in your bot |
| **Tidewater Crew** | Drowned Docks | Crane-arm hydraulics, clamp hands, grappling | Dockworkers' union turned syndicate | Smuggle parts; fight their grudge matches |
| **The Choir** | Kiln Row chapels | Scripture-welded plates, salvaged cores | Believe a destroyed bot's core holds its "voice"; they collect heads | Bring them heads. Or else. |
| **Grey Market** | Everywhere | Fixers, not fighters: mods, intel, betting books | Money | Throw a fight; pay back a debt |

### 4.3 Independents — "the Garages"

| Group | Who | Tech |
|---|---|---|
| **Salvagers' Co-op (Junkline)** | Scrap reconditioners; the starter-parts brand | Cheap, rugged, unreliable, everywhere |
| **Veterans' Union** | Ex-demolition and crane operators | Surplus Hollis frames, old-school boxing sense |
| **Garage crews** | Families, kids, dreamers — **you** | Whatever they can afford |

### 4.4 The officials

- **FBRBA** — sanctioning body. Inspectors check for illegal mods before Regional and Grand Circuit fights. They have weight classes, judges, and a human referee with a ref drone.
- **Ferrum Bay PD Robotics Unit** — raids illegal pits. Their heat rises when you fight on gang cards.

### 4.5 How "sides" work for the player

The player starts **Independent**. Five reputation tracks move with every choice:
**Makers** (as a group, plus a per-maker contract), **Crews** (per crew), **FBRBA
standing**, **Crowd** (fame), and **Heat** (police attention).

- **Sponsor contract (Maker):** discounted parts and a salary, but you must run their livery and parts and follow their brand rules ("no destroying in televised fights", "win by decision bonus"). Breaking a rule costs money and standing.
- **Crew affiliation:** illegal mods, underground purses, betting income, protection — but FBRBA inspections can disqualify you, Heat rises, and crews ask for favors (take a dive in round 3, destroy a rival's bot, carry parts).
- **Stay Independent:** hardest economy, freest choices, the underdog story.

Story branches open from these tracks: a Maker scouting offer, a Crew debt, the
Choir's interest in Ironmonger's heads, a Grey Market fixed fight.

## 5. Leagues and tournaments

| Tier | League | Sanction | Rules (summary) | Format |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pit Nights** (The Yards) | None, Rustmouth-run | Anything goes: clinch, headbutt, stomp on a downed bot. No weight classes. Destroy allowed. | Weekly cards; 3-fight win streak earns a District invite |
| 2 | **District Circuit** | Crew-run, semi-legal | Clinch up to 3 s; headbutts banned; destroy allowed | Four venues; 8-bot single-elimination brackets, damage carries over between rounds |
| 3 | **FBRBA Regionals** | Sanctioned | Boxing only (punches above the hip line); illegal mods = DQ; 3 weight classes; destroy only after KO, fined | Ranked ladder plus a seasonal 16-bot bracket |
| 4 | **Grand Circuit** | Sanctioned + televised | Strict rules, judges, inspections, sponsor obligations | Invitational round-robin to a title fight for the belt |

Weight classes (sanctioned tiers): **Light** < 260 kg, **Middle** 260–340 kg,
**Heavy** 340–420 kg, **Super-heavy** > 420 kg.

Side events: grudge matches, exhibitions, charity fights, and later **Scrap Royale** (four bots, one cage).

## 7. Season 1 story arc (for the game and the Shorts series)

1. **Pit 4.** Ironside debuts against Rustbucket Rex. Mang Benny promises your uncle's bot will fight again.
2. **The Rustmouths' cut.** Win three Pit Nights. Big Ferro demands a share, and Scrapjack beats you to the District invite.
3. **Districts.** Copperline offers overvolt mods (Heat). Tidewater wants a grudge match. Hush's owner offers money to lose to Coinflip.
4. **The sponsor.** Kestrel scouts you after you beat Needle. The contract says no destroys and Kestrel parts only.
5. **Regionals.** FBRBA inspections. Cardinal Rule and the Choir show interest in the heads you take, or the ones you spare.
6. **Scrapjack's fall.** Your rival loses his bot to Ironmonger and asks for help. Help him, or take his parts.
7. **Grand Circuit.** Duchess, Monolith, Stratos, Overcurrent. Ironmonger's corner is revealed to be the Choir.
8. **Title fight.** Ironmonger. Sabre unveils Sovereign as the "real champion" in the post-fight cliffhanger.

Every arc beat is designed to work as a 30–60 s Short on its own. See overhaul-plan.md §12.
