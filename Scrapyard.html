    How to play

    The idea

    • Build a bot in the Garage, or weld your own head and chassis in the Workshop.
    • Win purses in the Career to buy better parts, crews, chips and signature moves.
    • Beat 3 bots in a league to open the next one. The Foreman waits at the top.

    Coach mode

    • Your bot fights on its own style and game plan.
    • 1 Head hunt · 2 Body work · 3 Cover up · 4 Haymaker
    • Each call has a cooldown. Time them to openings.

    Pilot mode

    • AD or arrows move · WS change lanes
    • J jab · K cross · L hook · I uppercut
    • 🎮 Controller: stick or d-pad moves, A jab, B cross, X hook, Y uppercut, LB/RB slip, LT guard, RT signature, Back switches Coach/Pilot, Start pauses. In Coach mode A/B/X/Y are the four calls. In menus A selects and B goes back.
    • Local versus (main menu): two players, one keyboard or two controllers. The legend at the bottom of the fight shows both players' keys.
    • X clinch when you are close: tie up to recover battery and heat, the referee breaks it after a second or two
    • QE slip · hold Shift to guard · F signature move
    • Press C in a fight to switch between Coach and Pilot.

    Tells and signatures

    • Every bot has a signature move. A red banner shows its tell, like "SHOULDER COCKS · HIGH HAYMAKER".
    • Slip or cover when you see it. A grey banner is a feint: do not bite.

    Parts and rarity

    • Every part has a rarity: Scrap, Street, Pro, Prototype and Illegal. Rarer parts cost more and have stronger traits.
    • The Underground Scrap parts each do one special thing, shown in green on the card, with the catch in red.
    • Illegal parts work anywhere, but in the Regional and Pit leagues inspectors can overturn a win and keep the purse.

    Damage and conditions

    • The outline beside each name shows every part: green, amber, red, dashed when torn off.
    • Damaged parts can develop conditions: oil spills (slower hands, a slick on the mat), broken gears (slow or jamming limbs), sensor glitches (coach calls lost or late), coolant leaks, power drain, loose plating, servo stutter, gyro wobble.
    • Calling 3 Cover up reboots a glitched head once per round. Your crew fixes any condition with one weld between rounds: tap the part on the outline.

    Knockdowns

    • When you go down, your vision blurs and a reboot dial appears.
    • Press Space or tap when the needle is in the green. Each hit brings systems back. Misses cost time.
    • Get enough hits before the ref reaches ten. Three knockdowns in a round is a TKO.

    Corner and finishers

    • Between rounds your crew welds damaged parts. Spend welds where they matter, then ring the bell.
    • After a stoppage the winner chooses: D destroy for a crowd bonus and salvage, or S spare for sportsmanship pay.
    • Esc pauses the fight.
